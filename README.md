# 🌆 Ecopolis: Smart City & Sustainable Infrastructure Model

A working hardware prototype developed for the Project Expo at **V.S.B. Engineering College, Karur** by the **Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) – Girls Batch (2025–2029)**. This collaborative project demonstrates integrated urban infrastructure combining renewable energy, automated traffic management, and smart building utilities.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Modules

* **☀️ Solar-Powered Smart Street Lighting:** Uses a solar panel to harvest light energy and power LED streetlights along an elevated highway/flyover loop.
* **🚦 Automated Traffic Control System:** Functional microcontroller-based traffic signal light (Red, Yellow, Green) managing multi-level road intersections.
* **🏢 Smart Commercial Building & Helipad:** Features a curved modern building model (**VSB**) equipped with a rooftop helipad (with motorized rotor), mist/cooling diffuser, and an automated mini water fountain in the courtyard.

---

## 🛠️ Components & Materials Used

* **Electronics & Hardware:**
  * Solar Panel
  * Microcontroller (Arduino Uno / Nano)
  * LEDs (Red, Yellow, Green, White)
  * DC Motor (for helicopter rotor)
  * Mini Submersible Water Pump
  * Ultrasonic Mist Maker Module
  * Jumper Wires, Switches & Battery/Power Supply
* **Structural & Craft Materials:**
  * Foam board, cardboard, acrylic paints, miniature vehicles, and decorative landscaping elements.

## 🚀 Setup & Operation

1. **Traffic Control Circuit:** Connect the Red, Yellow, and Green signal LEDs to the microcontroller output pins as configured in the control logic.
2. **Solar Lighting:** Connect the solar panel output to the rechargeable battery and streetlamp LED circuit.
3. **Smart Utilities:** Power on the switches for the rooftop rotor motor, courtyard water fountain pump, and mist diffuser module.
